# Selkent Recycling Limited Website

A simple, static website for a small family-run furniture, white goods, removals and clearance business in Kent. It lists the services offered and lets customers send an enquiry through a contact form.

**Live site:** _(add link once deployed)_

## What it does

- Presents the business and its services (removals, clearances, furniture and white goods, social services support such as deep cleans and decluttering)
- Shows contact details
- Provides an enquiry form (name, address, email, preferred date, services needed, extra details) that emails the business owner

## How it's built

- Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript (no framework, no database)
- Hosted free on GitHub Pages, deployed automatically from this repo
- Form submissions handled by a third-party form service and emailed to the owner
- Spam protection: honeypot field plus CAPTCHA, and submissions restricted to the site's own domain
