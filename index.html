Our services

Removals

Moving day made easy. With over 20 years of experience, our team will shift anything from a single wardrobe to a full house, with affordable services to match.

Clearances

Whether you're downsizing, relocating or settling the affairs of a loved one, our team clears properties of every size efficiently, and with dignity, care and respect. You can trust us with the keys: if there's something you've lost or can't find, we'll keep a lookout and make sure it comes back to you.

Furniture and white goods

Pop into our shop in Twydall for pre-loved furniture, wardrobes, chests of drawers and white goods, all without the new-furniture price tag. Our white goods are fully tested, PAT tested and working to a high standard, and every appliance comes with a 4 month guarantee. Looking for something new? We can supply new beds and mattresses too. And there's plenty more to rummage through: books, CDs, records and all sorts of treasures.

Deep cleaning and decluttering

A fresh start for homes that have been struggling. We work patiently and discreetly, at a pace that suits you, until the place feels clean, clear and comfortable again.

Working with councils and social services

We work closely with the council and social services, and have experience supporting vulnerable clients across all of our services: removals, deep cleaning, decluttering, and furniture and white goods. Whatever the situation, we're kind, discreet and understanding.

About us

Selkent Recycling, trading as Archies Appliances, is a family-run business owned by two brothers, Archie and Albert. We have over 20 years of experience delivering services to our clients, and we have been based in Twydall for more than ten of those years. Along the way we have become part of the local community.

As well as helping private customers, we carry out clearances for landlords, estate agents, solicitors and executors.

Why customers choose us

  • Free, no-obligation quotes. For whole-house removals and for every clearance we visit to see the job and price it properly. For smaller removals we can often quote over the phone. There is no obligation to accept.
  • Everything disposed of responsibly. We never dump anything. Disposing of items properly costs money, and our quotes reflect that.
  • Insured. Your belongings and your property are covered.
  • Punctual, professional and hardworking. Honest, reliable and discreet, whatever the job.
  • Local deliveries. Bought something from our shop? We can deliver it locally.
  • A shop you can visit. We are open Monday to Friday and anyone is welcome to come and browse. If we haven't got what you're after, let us know and we'll keep an eye out for it.
