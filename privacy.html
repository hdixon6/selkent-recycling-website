Privacy notice

This explains what we do with the personal information you give us when you contact us through this website. We keep it simple, because we don't use your details for anything except dealing with your enquiry.

Who we are

Selkent Recycling Limited, trading as Archies Appliances, company number 08772642. Registered office: 2 Charles Court, Avenue Road, Erith, DA8 3BY. Shop: 3 Twydall Green, Gillingham, ME8 6JY. Email: selkent2009@yahoo.co.uk. We decide how your information is used, so we are the "data controller".

What we collect

When you use our enquiry form: your name, the address of the job, your email address, your phone number (if you give it), the date you would like the work done, the services you need, and anything you tell us about the job.

If you hire us: your name and address and the details of the work, so that we can invoice you.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and give you a quote.
  • To carry out and invoice the work if you go ahead.
  • To keep the business records we are required by law to keep.

We do not use your details for marketing, and we do not sell or share them with anyone for their own purposes.

Who sees it

  • Us. Your enquiry is sent to our business email account.
  • Our form service. We use a service called Web3Forms to deliver form submissions to our email. Your enquiry passes through its systems, and it keeps a copy for 30 days.
  • Our accountant. Invoices are passed to our accountant so that our accounts and tax can be dealt with.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries: we delete enquiry emails once they have been dealt with, and in any case within 12 months.
  • Invoices and accounting records: we keep these for as long as the law requires, which is six years from the end of the company financial year they relate to.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it (we will, unless the law requires us to keep it). To do any of these, email us at selkent2009@yahoo.co.uk or call the shop on 01634 393242.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.
